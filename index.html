About Us

A Church Moved by the
Message of the Cross

The Way Church (더웨이교회) is a Presbyterian Church in America (PCA) congregation located in the heart of Silicon Valley. Our name comes from Jesus' declaration in John 14:6 — "I am the way and the truth and the life."

We are a community committed to being clear in the Gospel (복음에 선명한). Through Christ-centered preaching, intergenerational discipleship, and authentic fellowship, we seek to grow together in faith and share the love of Jesus with our neighbors.

Our vision is to view all of Scripture through the lens of Christ and His redemptive work, building a church that is truly moved by the message of the cross.
